Earth textures from NASA (public domain). The day and night images come in two
sizes: the 8192 wide one where the device can take it, the 4096 wide one
elsewhere. `scripts/make-earth-textures.py` makes both from NASA's originals.

- `earth-blue-marble.jpg`, `earth-blue-marble-8k.jpg`: Blue Marble with shaded
  relief and bathymetry, from NASA GIBS
- `earth-night.jpg`, `earth-night-8k.jpg`: Black Marble 2016 city lights, NASA
  Earth Observatory
- `earth-water.png`: water mask (used for the sun glint), as packaged by the
  three-globe project
